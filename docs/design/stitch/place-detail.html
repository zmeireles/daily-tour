Lagoa do Fogo

Hiking Viewpoint

Situated in the center of the island of São Miguel, this crater lake is set within the Água de Pau Massif stratovolcano. It is the highest lake on the island, characterized by its pristine, untouched shores and dramatic, steep green walls. The area surrounding the lake is a protected nature reserve, ensuring the preservation of its unique endemic flora and fauna.
